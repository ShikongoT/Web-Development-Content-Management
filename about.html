About Us

Our History

Heritage House Museum was started by local historians and community leaders who wanted to protect the culture and history of the region. It began as a small collection of donated objects in one room. Today it has several galleries, and thousands of visitors, students and researchers come every year.

Over the years the museum has added old documents, photographs, cloth, tools and artworks. These show how our community has changed.

Our Purpose

The museum collects, looks after and studies important objects and records. We want everyone to be able to see them, learn from them and enjoy them.

Our Mission

To protect the objects that tell our community's story and to give visitors of all ages and backgrounds a chance to learn.

Our Vision

To be a friendly place where people can learn about history at any age, with good online resources and strong links to the community.

Our Values

  • Being honest when we show history
  • Making the museum open to everyone
  • Respecting different cultures
  • Always learning and trying new ideas
