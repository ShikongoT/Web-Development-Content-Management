About Us

Our History

Heritage House Museum was established by a group of local historians and community leaders who recognised the need to protect and celebrate the region's cultural heritage. Beginning as a small collection of donated artefacts housed in a single room, the museum has grown into a multi-gallery institution that welcomes thousands of visitors, students and researchers each year.

Over the decades, the museum has expanded its collections to include historical documents, photographs, textiles, tools and works of art that reflect the social, economic and cultural development of our community.

Our Purpose

Heritage House Museum exists to collect, preserve, research and interpret objects and records of lasting historical and cultural value, and to make them accessible to the public for education, inspiration and enjoyment.

Our Mission

To safeguard the material heritage of our community and provide meaningful, accessible educational experiences for visitors of all ages and backgrounds.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of heritage preservation and lifelong learning, recognised for its welcoming environment, innovative digital resources and commitment to the community it serves.

Our Values

  • Integrity in preserving and presenting history
  • Accessibility for all members of the community
  • Respect for cultural diversity and heritage
  • Continuous learning and innovation
