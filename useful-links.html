Useful Links

Below are external websites and resources that complement the information and collections found at Heritage House Museum. These links open in a new browser tab.

  • International Council of Museums (ICOM) - the global network of museum professionals and museum standards.
  • UNESCO World Heritage - information on world heritage sites and cultural preservation initiatives.
  • Library of Congress Digital Collections - a vast digital archive of historical photographs, documents and recordings.
  • Europeana - a digital platform providing access to millions of items from European museums and archives.
  • Smithsonian Institution - one of the world's largest museum, education and research complexes.
