Useful Links

These websites have more information about museums and history. The links open in a new tab.

  • International Council of Museums (ICOM) - a worldwide group for museum workers, which sets standards for museums.
  • UNESCO World Heritage - information about World Heritage sites and projects to protect culture.
  • Library of Congress Digital Collections - a very large online collection of old photos, documents and recordings.
  • Europeana - a website with millions of items from European museums and archives.
  • Museums of Namibia - a list of museums in Namibia, with links to their websites and contact details.

If you know a website that should be on this list, tell us through our contact form.
