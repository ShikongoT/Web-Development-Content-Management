Frequently Asked Questions

What are the museum's opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00, and Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays. You can also see the Home page.

How much does admission cost?

General admission is N$50 for adults and N$25 for students and pensioners. The Services page has all the prices.

Do I need to book a ticket in advance?

You do not need to book ahead for normal entry. It is a good idea to book for guided tours and school groups.

Is the museum accessible to visitors with disabilities?

Yes. Our galleries are wheelchair friendly. We have ramps, accessible toilets and reserved parking.

Can I take photographs inside the museum?

You can take photos without flash in most galleries. Some items borrowed from other museums may not be photographed. These are marked with signs.

Do you offer educational programmes for schools?

Yes. We run workshops and guided programmes for school groups. See the Services page for details.

How can I access the digital archive?

You can find our scanned photos, e-books and other online items on the Digital Resources page.

Can I donate an item to the museum's collection?

We are happy to hear about donations. Please use the Contact & Feedback page to tell us about the item, and our curators will reply.

How do I book a research appointment?

You can ask for an appointment using the contact form. The Research Support page has more information.
