Our Services

The museum offers several services for visitors, schools, researchers and the community.

Guided Tours

Guided tours of our main galleries every day, led by trained guides.

Educational Programmes

Workshops and school visits that match what students learn in class.

Group Bookings

Cheaper prices for tour groups and clubs.

Venue Hire

Hold your event in our hall, surrounded by history and art.

Exhibition Loans

We lend some objects and travelling exhibitions to approved museums and institutions.

Conservation Advice

Tips for people and organisations on how to look after old objects and documents.
