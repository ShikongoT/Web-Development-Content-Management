Our Services

Heritage House Museum offers a range of services for visitors, schools, researchers and the wider community.

Guided Tours

Daily guided tours of our permanent galleries led by knowledgeable museum docents.

Educational Programmes

Curriculum-linked workshops and school visits designed for learners of all ages.

Group Bookings

Special arrangements and discounted rates for tour groups, clubs and societies.

Venue Hire

Host your event in our heritage function hall, surrounded by history and art.

Exhibition Loans

We loan selected artefacts and travelling exhibitions to accredited institutions.

Conservation Advice

Guidance for individuals and organisations on caring for historical objects and documents.
